# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html`, deployed on Cloudflare Pages with Pages Functions in `functions/` and D1 for storage (repo template). Self-hosted fonts and scripts.

## Users

Independent and emerging artists in Byron Bay, the Northern Rivers and remotely. They arrive with voice memos, notebook lyrics, home demos or a melody stuck in their head. Many are recording professionally for the first time. They want to know whether Ari is the right person to turn their idea into a finished, release-ready track.

## Product Purpose

LevProductions is the studio of Ari Levy: producer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, sound engineer and performer. The site turns curious artists into discovery-call enquiries. Success means a visitor sends the contact form to book a free 15-minute discovery call.

## Positioning

One producer, one-on-one, in a private Byron Bay studio. Song-driven first and genre second. Mentoring is part of the work, and the studio is described as a community ("join the fam"). Packages are fixed-price with no hidden fees, and Ari takes ownership of getting the song finished.

## Operating Context

The journey: free 15-minute discovery call, then the first session is booked. Ari takes pre-written songs (Full Production) or co-writes from scratch (Co-Written Song). Mixing and mastering are offered in-house or outsourced, and remote file projects are accepted.
