06About Ari

A little about me and my work

I’m a producer, multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, sound engineer and performer currently located in the northern rivers. I have over 10 years of experience working alongside artists to bring their music to life. My approach is all about collaboration - shaping songs together, supporting melodies and lyrics, and making sure the final track feels true to your vision.

I started out as a performing musician, playing shows across Sydney and at festivals, before moving into a production role where I now focus on working closely with artists. Along the way, I also ran a hip hop record label for four years, gaining valuable industry experience and a deep understanding of how to support artists both creatively and professionally.

With a background spanning in hip hop, neo-soul, funk, jazz, RnB and pop, I bring versatility to every project. Whether it’s co-writing, arranging, or building a track from scratch, I see every project as a partnership - combining your creativity with my technical expertise in production and composition to create music that truly connects.

07FAQs

FAQs

Take 00 / 10

Take 01How much does it cost to work with a music producer in the Northern Rivers?

Music production prices in the Northern Rivers vary depending on the project, but at LevProductions I offer flexible options for songwriting support, recording, mixing and mastering. Prices depend on session length and project scope, and you can book or request a quote directly through my website.

Take 02What does a music producer do for artists?

A music producer helps artists develop their sound by supporting songwriting, arrangement, recording, editing, mixing and overall creative direction. At LevProductions, I collaborate closely with artists to take songs from rough ideas or voice notes to polished, release-ready tracks.

Take 03What genres do you work in?

Pop, soul, R&B, neo-soul, indie pop, lo-fi, funk, folk, groove-driven music — that's the world I live in. But honestly, genre is secondary. I'm song-driven first. Whatever the track needs to feel right, that's what we go after. If it serves the song, it belongs in the room.

Take 04Do you offer mixing and mastering for songs recorded at home?

Yes. If you've recorded vocals or instruments at home, I can mix and master your track to professional release quality. You can book a mixing or mastering session online, and I accept remote project files from anywhere.

Take 05How does a session work?

We start with a free 15-minute discovery call to talk about your music, what you're going for, and whether we're a good fit. From there we book your first session and get to work. Simple as that.

Take 06Can I bring an unfinished song idea to a producer?

Yes — producers often work with unfinished ideas. At LevProductions, you can bring a voice memo, lyrics, a melody or any rough start. I help develop the structure, write melodies or lyrics, and build the full production around your idea.

Take 07How long will my project take?

Don't worry about counting sessions — that's my job. When you book a package, I take full ownership of getting your music to where it needs to be. You'll walk away with something you're genuinely proud of. Check out the packages to find the right fit, or book a free discovery call and we'll figure it out together.

Take 08What should I bring to my first session?

Yourself, your ideas, and any references — songs you love, voice memos, demos you've made at home. The more I understand what you're going for, the better we can get there.

Take 09Is LevProductions just for experienced artists?

Not at all. Many of my clients are recording professionally for the first time. Part of what I do is mentoring — helping you understand the process, trust your instincts, and leave feeling confident about your music.

Take 10Where are you located?

The studio is based in Byron Bay, NSW in the Northern Rivers. It's a private, comfortable space designed for focused creative work. Remote projects are also welcome — send your files and we'll take it from there.
