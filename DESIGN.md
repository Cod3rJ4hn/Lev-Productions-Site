# Design System: LevProductions

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Wall, One Piece"**

Each section is a wall with a single painted moment; the rest stays bare. Walls are concrete: cream for reading, black for the work, one orange wall for the discovery call.

## Colors

### Primary
- **Signal Orange** (#F53C17): paint and primary actions. As text on cream it is large lettering only.

### Neutral
- **Session Cream** and **Control-Room Black** are the two walls. **Studio Brown** is secondary text on cream; **Tape Sand** and **Dust Grey** are secondary text on black. **Error Salmon** is for form errors.

## Typography

Rubik Spray Paint is the lettering (uppercase; the font file is subset to capitals, figures and basic punctuation). Sedgwick Ave Display is the handstyle for short tags and artist names. Bricolage Grotesque sets everything that must be read, including list prices.

**The Headlines-Only Rule.** Sprayed lettering never goes below about 1.1rem and never sets a sentence of body copy.

## Layout

1280px wrapper, fluid gutter, 72-128px section padding. Hairlines and bare wall instead of cards. Pinned sections apply from 1000px wide and 720px tall; below that they stack or scroll natively.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat paint on textured walls. Only the floating Spotify player has a shadow.

## Shapes

Square controls and photographs. Artist posters use a torn-edge mask and slight rotation.

## Components

- **Buttons:** flat orange or black; hover lifts and tilts, active scales to 0.97, focus is a 3px outline.
- **Services lanes:** a spray can travels across and lays down each waveform, using two counter-sliding layers so only transforms animate.
- **Pricing:** accessible tabs and native accordions. Without JavaScript every tab is shown in full.
- **Artists:** wheat-pasted posters on a pinned sideways shelf.
- **Form:** underlined fields on black.

Paint artwork and wall textures are generated by `tools/make-textures.mjs`.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** keep one painted moment per section.
- **Do** keep policy, prices and body copy in Bricolage.
- **Don't** hide content for an entrance animation.
- **Don't** add colours, autoplaying audio or smooth-scroll (it breaks pin measurement).
